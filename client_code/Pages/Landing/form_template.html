Up and running in three steps

  1. 1
    Start a family board

    Make an account, name your board and pick an avatar. It takes about a minute.

  2. 2
    Invite your people

    Everyone else scans the board's QR code or types its 6-letter code. Up to 4 people per board.

  3. 3
    Plan, pitch in, earn

    Add tasks, events and wants. Finish tasks to earn brownie points and level up.

One board, three kinds of cards

Tasks

Chores and to-dos. Give one to someone or leave it open, set a due date and priority, and add brownie points as a reward.

Events

Plans with a date, time and place. Everyone taps “I'm in” so you know who's coming.

Wants

Wishes for the family or for one person, with an optional link. Others can +1 the ones they like too.

Each card gets a short key like FAM-12, so it's easy to talk about in chat.

Small touches that keep everyone on the same page

Bounties anyone can claim

Put points on a task and leave it open. Whoever claims it and finishes it gets the points.

Optional approval

Mark a task as needing a check. When it's done, someone else approves it before the points are paid.

Repeating tasks and turns

Repeat daily, on weekdays, weekly, every 2 weeks or monthly. Turn on “Take turns” to pass the job around.

A board for the whole family

Cards move from To do to In progress, Review and Done. Filter by type, your own cards, or open bounties.

Family chat

Chat together and see a feed of what got done. Type FAM-12 to link a card. Every card has its own comments too.

See who's seen it

Every card shows who has read it, who's coming, or who +1'd it. No more “I didn't know”.

Levels and a podium

Climb from Rookie to Mythic, chase a weekly family goal, and see who's on top this week.

Phone, computer, day or night

Made for phones first, with a wider board on computers and a dark night mode for evenings.

Brownie points: why the chores actually get done

  • Add points to a task. Whoever finishes it earns them.
  • If a task needs approval, the points are paid once someone else says it's done.
  • Points add up to levels, a weekly family goal, and a podium for the week and for all time.
  • Reopen a finished card and its points are taken back, so the score stays fair.

Good to know

Who is it for?

Households of 2 to 4 people: parents and kids, couples, or roommates. Each board holds up to 4 people.

Does everyone need to install an app?

No. Fun To Plan runs in the web browser on phones and computers. Each person makes their own account and joins with the board's code.

Who can see our board?

Only the people on your board. Every change goes through the server, which checks that you belong to the family first.

How do we get started without a blank board?

When you create a board you can add a few starter cards that show how things work. Delete them whenever you like.

What if someone leaves?

Anyone can leave a board and rejoin later with the code, and they keep their brownie points. The person who made the board can also remove members.
